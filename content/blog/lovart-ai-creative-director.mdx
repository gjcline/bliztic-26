# This week's hot pick

**Lovart AI - global launch announced July 23 2025**  
Meet your new autopilot creative director

## What it does ✨

- Takes a single brief and writes the whole campaign plan
- Chooses the best model for each task. Example: VEO3 for video, Ideogram for logos
- Keeps every asset in one shared canvas so feedback is as easy as typing or sketching

## Creative revenue ideas

### Brand-in-a-Day workshop

- Morning discovery call with the founder. Define mission, tone, colors, audience.
- Mid-day: feed the brief into Lovart. It generates a logo, color guide, hero video, ten social tiles, and a one-page brand sheet.
- Afternoon polish. Tweak colors or wording live while the client watches.
- Evening Zoom reveal. Deliver a Google Drive folder plus Figma link. Collect full payment before hand-off.
- Price anchor: 4 000 USD for solo founders. Add 1 500 USD for a next-day business-card run and Canva kit.

### Live storyboard selling

- Join a discovery call with a prospect who needs ads. Paste their concept into Lovart in real time.
- Lovart outputs a shot-by-shot storyboard, a 15-second teaser cut, suggested color grading, and recommended music.
- Use screen-share so the prospect witnesses assets materialize. Ask: "Do you want us to finish this by Friday or Tuesday?"
- Close on the spot with a 30 percent deposit. Upsell motion graphics or voice-over once the teaser is approved.

### Subscription content engine

- Offer a monthly package: four mini campaigns for every new product or promotion.
- Your workload: one hour of prompting per client each week. Lovart produces Instagram reels, YouTube Shorts, product-demo GIFs, and matching thumbnails.
- **Tiered pricing:**
  - Starter: 1 500 USD per month for two campaigns
  - Growth: 3 000 USD per month for four campaigns
  - Scale: 5 000 USD per month for unlimited drafts plus quarterly brand refresh
- Because Lovart handles volume, you keep margins above 70 percent without hiring designers or editors.
