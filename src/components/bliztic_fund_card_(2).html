What the fund covers

The fund covers the initial development and implementation costs associated with the Full Stack Sales Engine. All build costs are absorbed upfront so your engagement begins with a fully operational system already in place.

How core clients qualify

Core client slots are limited to five at any time. Availability is first come, first served for companies that meet our fit criteria: B2B companies with active revenue, an identifiable growth gap, and a decision maker ready to move.

What happens after go-live

Once your engine is live, you enter the ongoing operating agreement where Bliztic manages, runs, and optimizes the system on a weekly, monthly, and quarterly basis. That is where the real return is built. The fund gets you in. The operating agreement is where we grow together.

Common questions
Is this a grant, loan, or equity deal?

None of the above. The Bliztic Fund is an internal client incentive. We absorb the implementation cost so you can get started without an upfront payment. No equity, no debt, no external program. Your ongoing agreement with Bliztic is the only commitment required.

What does the ongoing agreement look like?

After implementation, Bliztic operates your Sales Engine on an ongoing basis. Weekly execution management, system optimization, performance oversight, and monthly and quarterly reviews are all included. Pricing is based on scope and is covered during the qualification process.

What if a slot isn't available right now?

You can still get started through the standard implementation agreement, which covers the same scope with an upfront development fee. Alternatively, we can add you to the priority list for the next available funded slot.
